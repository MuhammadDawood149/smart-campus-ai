      Heuristic
      h(n) = k · euclid(n, goal), k = 1/3

      Straight-line distance alone would overestimate on this map: Main_Gate–Bus_Stop is 3 units apart but costs only 1. Scaling by k = 1/3, the smallest cost-per-distance ratio of any edge, keeps h(n) at or below the real cost of every edge, so h is admissible and consistent and A* stays optimal.
